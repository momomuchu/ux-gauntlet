- **requirements.txt drifts stale behind its own applied fixes.** Both F49 (BLOCKED trigger) and F170 (sole-input claim) show requirements.txt carrying a CR-tag claiming a fix was applied while the actual reconciling clause only landed in spec.md or nowhere at all. spec.md is functioning as the more-current SSOT in practice; requirements.txt needs a sync pass before freeze, not just per-round patching.
