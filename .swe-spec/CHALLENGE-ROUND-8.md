## 4. Systemic observations

- **High re-litigation rate this round.** 3 of 16 confirmed findings (F192 ×2, F83) reconstruct defects already found and explicitly DECLINED or deferred in scrub-log.md CR7/DR-28, without engaging the prior disposition. All three were correctly downgraded from BLOCKER/MAJOR claims to MINOR on verification, but the pattern signals a need for lenses to grep scrub-log.md before filing, not just after.
- **Verification is doing real work, not rubber-stamping.** 6 of 16 confirmed items had a severity_claim (BLOCKER or MAJOR from the attacking lens) revised down after checking scrub-log/tests/existing disclosures — evidence the panel has teeth in both directions, catching overclaiming as readily as underclaiming.
